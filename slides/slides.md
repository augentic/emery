###### PROPELLERHEAD · THE EXPOSURE

# Generated code is plausible by default

<div class="slide-lead">Speed will not rescue a fuzzy strategy. Unbounded citizen coding turns fast prototypes into material risk.</div>

<div class="exposure-grid">
<div class="cc-card">
<div class="card-eyebrow"><span class="card-num">01 · DATA &amp; SECRETS</span><span>BOUNDARY LOSS</span></div>
<h3>Data leaves the boundary</h3>
<p>Proprietary data, credentials and customer records leak into personal accounts, unapproved models and third-party services.</p>
</div>
<div class="cc-card">
<div class="card-eyebrow"><span class="card-num">02 · ACCOUNTABILITY</span><span>NO AUDIT TRAIL</span></div>
<h3>Chat history is not an audit trail</h3>
<p>Apps run on personal devices with no accountable owner, no durable record of business rules and no operational support.</p>
</div>
<div class="cc-card">
<div class="card-eyebrow"><span class="card-num">03 · ASSURANCE</span><span>PROMPT LUCK</span></div>
<h3>Quality depends on the builder</h3>
<p>Security, privacy and architectural coherence are left to individual prompt luck instead of deterministic checks.</p>
</div>
<div class="cc-card">
<div class="card-eyebrow"><span class="card-num">04 · PRODUCTION GAP</span><span>STALLED VALUE</span></div>
<h3>Worthwhile ideas stall</h3>
<p>A working demo proves possibility, but there is no clean route from a fragile prototype to an operable production system.</p>
</div>
</div>

###### PROPELLERHEAD · LEGACY MODERNISATION

# AI makes rewriting cheap enough<br>to be dangerous

<div class="modernisation-grid">
<div>
<div class="phl-tagline-box" style="grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 16px; margin-bottom: 24px;">
<p><strong>AI can survey source material and generate a new implementation quickly. It cannot decide what should survive.</strong> Recovery of intent is the scarce work.</p>
<p>The same spec-driven pipeline that turns a citizen app into production software untangles the legacy systems nobody fully understands any more.</p>
</div>
<div class="hero-pillars" style="grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 18px;">
<div class="hero-pillar"><span class="hero-pillar-num">EMERY</span><span>Separates business behaviour from old platform machinery</span></div>
<div class="hero-pillar"><span class="hero-pillar-num">OMNIA</span><span>Replaces fragile runtimes in bounded production slices</span></div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="modernisation-steps">
<div class="modernisation-step">
<div class="card-eyebrow" style="margin-bottom: 2px;"><span class="card-num">01 · RECOVER INTENT</span><span>EVIDENCE</span></div>
<h4>Mine the specification from existing code and docs</h4>
<p>Extract what the system actually does today and surface conflicts or missing knowledge as explicit <code>[unknown]</code> gaps.</p>
</div>
<div class="modernisation-step">
<div class="card-eyebrow" style="margin-bottom: 2px;"><span class="card-num">02 · RE-EXPRESS</span><span>DURABLE SPEC</span></div>
<h4>Review behaviour, tests and target design</h4>
<p>Decide what to keep, retire or change before a single line of replacement code is trusted.</p>
</div>
<div class="modernisation-step">
<div class="card-eyebrow" style="margin-bottom: 2px;"><span class="card-num">03 · REPLACE SAFELY</span><span>NO BIG-BANG REWRITE</span></div>
<h4>Rebuild in bounded, verifiable slices</h4>
<p>Cut over increment by increment on a modern, capability-sandboxed runtime with independent verification.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>

###### PROPELLERHEAD · START SMALL

# Start with something real.<br>Make the next thing easier.

<div class="opportunity-rows" style="margin-top: 8px;">
<div class="opportunity-row">
<div class="opportunity-row-num">01</div>
<div>
<h4>Do not start with a company-wide AI programme</h4>
<p>Start with one team, a non-production sandbox and one useful problem that should be faster or easier than it is today.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="opportunity-row">
<div class="opportunity-row-num">02</div>
<div>
<h4>Get it live. Keep it working.</h4>

</div>
</div>
<div class="opportunity-row">
<div class="opportunity-row-num">03</div>
<div>
